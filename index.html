FAQ

Good to know.

What should I include in my brief?

Include where you’ll use the artwork, the look you have in mind, reference images, and any deadline.

What determines the final price?

The final quote depends on the scope, complexity, turnaround, and how you’ll use the artwork.

Can I use the art for merch?

Include merch or any other commercial use in your brief so the usage terms can be agreed on up front.
